Why ours is better

  • Profits in down markets. Crosswake's BTC filter keeps it in cash during bear markets; our long/short trend made money in 2022 and in 2025–26 while BTC fell.
  • Risk-based sizing. Every position is sized by its volatility and the whole book targets 20% annual vol — subscribers get weights, not just "buy X".
  • Higher bar of proof. Deflated Sharpe, probability of backtest overfitting and an SPA test are rarely run by retail tools; they are what caught the decay shown below.
  • Transparent costs. Every dollar of fees, spread, impact and funding is itemised; the edge survives 1.5× costs.
  • No survivorship bias. LUNA and FTT are in the test, crashes included.

Where Crosswake is ahead (be honest with subscribers)

  • It is already live-paper-trading with a public log — that is the only evidence subscribers should trust. Ours starts the day you run engine live step.
  • Simpler rules are easier to follow and harder to overfit; long-only spot is legal and usable for more people than leveraged perp shorts.
  • Larger universe (36 coins) gives more trades and statistical power.

4 · Recommendation

  1. Signal Trend only (original config). Keep breakout/xsmom off until they prove themselves in paper trading; keep carry as a small, cost-gated sleeve.
  2. Start the public paper track record now (uv run engine live step hourly) and post every signal including losses — the same thing that makes Crosswake credible.
  3. Run 3–6 months before subscribers. Expect live Sharpe ~0.5–0.9, drawdowns of 15–25%, and flat stretches.
  4. Offer a long-only/spot variant for subscribers who cannot or should not short perps with leverage.
